## Scenario

`spawn_mixed(n)`: 92 % ground units in groups of 10, each group in a lane of its own,
driving 12 km north at 6 m/s; 4 % ships in moving pairs; 2 % aircraft (half
helicopters); 2 % statics; all USA. The 500 run moves every ground group; the 1,200 and
2,000 runs move one ground group in five and hold the rest, alive (S10 ruling: DCS
itself falls to about 1 fps with 1,840 moving ground units). Every object is on one
coalition, so no AI detects another. Whether route shape changes DCS's cost is
UNVERIFIED (S15's E37).

## How the mirror is compared

The compare starts once the mirror holds as many rows as DCS holds objects (the
oracle's own count), with a deadline of twice the births' expected time. It fails on:
a missing or extra object, apart from objects the oracle names in transit; a position
farther from the oracle's than the larger of the present speed and the type's top
speed (`getDesc().speedMax`) allows over the sample's age, plus 1 m; a position
sample older than twice `P`, where `P` is the position period for an object that
moves, five times it for one that does not, and 60 s (the enumeration period) for a
static that cannot move; a life or fuel sample older than twice the vitals period for a
row a subscription names, or twelve times that for any other row; a life that
differs, or a fuel past its drift (0.002 a second of age, plus 0.001). Each period is
the tier's target, or the period the agent last reported when that is longer;
`--strict` holds to the targets and is the rule at 500 objects only. A moving
object's position older than twice `P` passes once, counted as `late`, when it is no
older than six periods, the oracle's early read found the object halted, and the
sample lies within 1.5 m of where it stood. A tier that owes rows and reports a
period of 0 fails. With a recording, the median interval between an object's last two
samples must lie within 25 % of the reported period (positions of moving objects,
life and fuel of subscribed rows; fewer than 20 objects are reported and not
judged). A dump with no `real` time or no five periods is an error. At a tenth of the
slice the ages and intervals are reported and not failed on, because the rates are
meant to fall there (S10 ruling 3b). The `cost_positions` check of twice the row cost
is judged against `sweep_row_us`.

6. The static classifier stays on: the run had no static mismatch (`statics_ok`), but it reached only 1 of the 3 required LIVE weapon kills of statics (the bombing task is UNVERIFIED). The next LIVE run adds the other 2.
7. The respawn rule ("a dead member is never spawned again") is not checked LIVE: no sidecar respawn policy ran in this run. It is covered on sim-fake only (`crates/sim-fake/tests/late_death.rs`, `crates/sim-fake/tests/dst.rs`).
